## Fire control, ATGM launchers and stabilization

Changes since **0.1.1**:

- A generic square ATGM launcher with one initial missile, plus a finite nearby ammunition box.
- Optical/manual laser/automatic laser rangefinders, APFSDS ballistic ranging, internal FCS linked to external sights, new thermal heads and T72 style sight, scalable clear/tinted/yellow glass and icons.
- Stronger adjustable elevation and turret drives in the elevation-drive inspector.
- Mirrored bustle feed arm and concise armour protection tooltips.
- Native Era/Technology dates and engine pricing, with compatible migration of existing settings and vehicles.

**Full pack:** SprocketColdWarExpansionPack-v0.2.0.zip includes all ten modules and the shared API. **Core only:** SprocketColdWarCore-v0.2.0.zip supplies the core and editable engine/cannon/era templates. Choose one; the loader remains separate.

Requires Sprocket 0.2.55.5 / BepInEx 6 IL2CPP 6.0.0-be.788. Close the game, back up saves and preserve custom configs/profiles/native data. Use the included installer to preview the update. Existing settings take precedence; see README for enabling automatic ammo-box feed after an update, FCS linking and rollback.
